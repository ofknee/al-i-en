# AL-I-EN
(puns puns puns and yes another eye game)

tw: creepy eyes and eyestrain

## notes

### instructions
1. spam "free eye" because you're broke
2. get money from any merges that occur
3. a. keep spamming free eyes
3. b. strategically buy eyes of your choice to fall in the general vicinity of your choice (right/left)
4. no you can't get mega rich and be an overlord because inflation :0
5. there is no height limit; however, if the jar overflows and an eye *falls*..... game over

- score is kept as a cumulative count of your lifetime's networth
- big eyes pop, making more room, so sticking to merging is defintiely in your interests
